Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don't, and who to ask — for readers of every age.

The short version

We collect almost nothing. Your game progress and reading history stay on your own device. There are no accounts and no chat. We never sell your information.

Who this policy is for

Our World is a site for everyone. Our content is written to be suitable for children, and grown-ups are welcome to read it too. This policy explains how we treat information for all visitors, and it calls out the extra protections we apply to children in a separate section below.

What your device stores

Games and reading notes save small things — a high score, the last page you opened, your light or dark theme choice — in your browser's localStorage. That is storage on your own machine, not on our server. Those notes never leave your device, and we cannot read them. If you clear your browser data, they are gone for good.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no passwords.
  • No chat or messaging.
  • No profiles, and no tracking of which pages you read.
  • No advertising identifiers set by us.
  • We do not ask anyone — child or adult — for personal information in order to use the site.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our World itself sets no cookies for its own purposes, and the site works fully without them. Cookies and similar technologies can, however, be set by the advertising services described below once advertising is live. Where the law requires it, those cookies are only set after you consent, and you can change or withdraw that consent at any time from the consent notice shown on the site.

Advertising

Our World is free, and advertising is what keeps it free. Two situations matter here:

  • While the site carries no advertising, no advertising cookies are set and this section does not apply.
  • Once advertising is live, ads are served by Google and its advertising partners. Those partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance and to decide which ads to show.

Advertising may be personalised (chosen using an inferred interest profile) or non-personalised (chosen without one, using only the page you are on and broad location). Personalised advertising is used only where it is lawful and, where required, only with your consent. In regions that require it, a consent notice lets you accept or refuse, and refusing still lets you use every part of the site.

You can read how Google handles data in its own policies: Google's advertising policies and Google's privacy policy. You can also manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com, and control cookies from any third party in your own browser settings.

Children's privacy

We take children's privacy seriously, and we have deliberately built this site so that there is very little to protect:

  • No personal information is collected from children. There is no sign-up, no chat, no comments and no user-generated content, so there is no place for a child to hand over a name, an email or anything else.
  • No behavioural advertising profiles are built for children. Where a child is using the site, we do not serve personalised advertising, and we do not use their activity to build an interest profile for advertising.
  • No precise location and no contact details are requested from anyone.
  • Content is written to be suitable for children, and frightening or graphic detail is edited out of news summaries.

If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, write to us at the address below and we will delete it.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a person under 18 is a child and processing their personal data requires verifiable parental consent. Under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws elsewhere, additional limits apply to sites directed at children. Because we do not collect personal data from children at all, we do not need to seek that consent — that is by design, not by accident.

Your rights

Because we hold almost nothing about you, most requests are easy to answer. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix anything inaccurate.
  • Deletion — ask us to erase your information.
  • Objection or restriction — object to certain processing, including profiling for advertising.
  • Withdraw consent — change your mind at any time, without losing access to the site.
  • Portability — receive your information in a portable format.
  • Non-discrimination — you are not treated worse for exercising any of these rights.

These rights come from frameworks including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended (CCPA/CPRA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”). To exercise any right, email us using the address below. Note that much of what you might want removed — game scores, theme choice — lives only on your own device, so clearing your browser data removes it immediately and we never had a copy.

Where you are in India, you also have the right to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity, and to complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Where you are in the EU or UK, you may complain to your local supervisory authority.

Data retention

We do not run a database of visitors, so there is no visitor record to retain. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of corrections, then delete it. Server and content-delivery logs created by our hosting provider are short-lived and are not used to build profiles of visitors.

Where your information goes

Our World is a static site served by a hosting provider and a content delivery network, so pages may be served from servers in several countries. If advertising is live, Google and its partners process data in the countries where they operate, under their own safeguards. We do not ourselves transfer personal information anywhere, because we do not hold any.

Keeping the site safe

The site is plain static files — no database, no accounts, nothing to break into. The main risk to you online is not this site; it is the other sites our links point to. We link out to sources so you can check facts, but those sites are not ours and have their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change how any of this works — particularly when advertising goes live — we will update this page and change the date below before the change takes effect. Continuing to use Our World after an update means you accept the updated policy.

We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade information about you, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising outside the advertising partners described above. Not now, and not if advertising changes.

Questions

Email us at contact@ourworld.eu.org and we will answer in plain words. A parent, teacher or young reader is equally welcome to write.

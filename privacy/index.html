Privacy

Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don't, and who to ask. In plain words.

The short version

We collect almost nothing. Your game progress and reading history stay on your own device. There are no accounts and no chat. We never sell your information.

What your device stores

Games and reading notes save small things — a high score, the last page you opened, your light or dark theme choice — in your browser's localStorage. Those notes never leave your device. If you clear your browser data, they are gone for good.

What we do not collect

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No chat or messaging.
  • No profiles, no tracking of what you read.
  • No contact form is live right now — the form on our About page does nothing until a real form address is set, so nothing you type into it leaves the page.

Ads, if they ever come

Today Our World shows no ads. We plan to add ads from Google one day. When that happens, Google and its partners may use cookies to choose which ads you see. We will update this page before that changes. You can read how Google handles data in its own policies: Google's advertising policies and Google's privacy policy.

We never sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade information about you. Not now, and not if ads arrive later.

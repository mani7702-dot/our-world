**WASP-189 b** (also known as **HD 133112 b**) is an [ultra-hot jupiter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultra-hot_Jupiter "Ultra-hot Jupiter") that orbits around the [A-type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-type_main-sequence_star "A-type main-sequence star") star WASP-189 which is located about 322 light years from Earth in the constellation [Libra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libra_(constellation) "Libra (constellation)"). It was first discovered in 2018, and was observed in 2020 by [CHEOPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CHEOPS "CHEOPS") (Characterising Exoplanets Satellite).

It has a mass of 1.99 Jupiters and a radius of 1.6 Jupiters. It orbits around a rapidly rotating A6IV-V star. It has a [polar orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_orbit "Polar orbit") around the star with a distance of 0.05 AU taking around 2.72 days to complete and orbit.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-EA-20200928-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-CHEOPS-2)

WASP-189 b was first discovered in 2018 using the [transit method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transit_method "Transit method").[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-4) In 2020, astronomers used [CHEOPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CHEOPS "CHEOPS") to observe it.[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-EA-20200928-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-CHEOPS-2)[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-A&A-20200917-5)

The planet has a mass of around 1.99 Jupiter masses with a radius of around 1.6 Jupiter radii.

The CHEOPS spacecraft was used to estimate the temperature of WASP-189 b. Assuming inefficient heat redistribution, the temperature of WASP-189 b was estimated to be around 3,200°C (5,790°F; 3,470 K). Assuming efficient heat redistribution and zero [albedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albedo "Albedo"), the temperature was calculated to be around 2,367°F (2,367°F; 1,570 K).[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-A&A-20200917-5) These observations would make WASP-189 b a ultra-hot jupiter and one of the [hottest known exoplanets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_hottest_exoplanets "List of hottest exoplanets") discovered to date.

Various spacecraft have been used to study the atmosphere of WASP-189 b such as CHEOPS and [CUTE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_Ultraviolet_Transit_Experiment "Colorado Ultraviolet Transit Experiment") (Colorado Ultraviolet Transit Experiment). The extreme temperatures of ultra-hot Jupiters such as WASP-189 b allow for the study of refractory elements that remain gaseous at high temperatures.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-:0-6)

Results show that the atmosphere contains many metal and volatile elements. CHEOPS observed [titanium oxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanium_oxide "Titanium oxide") (TiO) and [vanadium oxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanadium_oxide "Vanadium oxide") (VO) and a variety of [metals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal "Metal") such as [chromium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromium "Chromium") (Cr), [magnesium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesium "Magnesium") (Mg), [vanadium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanadium "Vanadium") (V) and [manganese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manganese "Manganese") (Mn). Singly ionized [Iron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron "Iron") (Fe, Fe+, Fe ll)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-8) and [Titanium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titanium "Titanium") (Ti, Ti+) were also found.[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-9) The [Immersion Grating Infrared Spectrometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immersion_Grating_Infrared_Spectrometer?action=edit&redlink=1 "Immersion Grating Infrared Spectrometer (page does not exist)") (IGRINS) on [Gemini South](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemini_South "Gemini South") detected neutral Iron (Fe I), [magnesium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnesium "Magnesium") (Mg I), [silicon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silicon "Silicon") (Si I), [water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water "Water") (H 2 O), [carbon monoxide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon_monoxide "Carbon monoxide") (CO) and [hydroxyl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydroxy_group "Hydroxy group") (OH).[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WASP-189b#cite_note-:0-6)

Parents & teachers

What we promise your kids

Our World is built quiet on purpose. Here is exactly what that means.

No ads. No trackers. No accounts. No chat.

  • No advertising — not around games, not inside games, not between posts.
  • No trackers or analytics — we do not use Google Analytics, ad pixels or fingerprinting. Fonts are served from our own files, so no third party sees who reads us.
  • No accounts — nothing to sign up for, no email collected, no profiles.
  • No chat or comments — there is no place on this site where a stranger can talk to a child.

Where game progress lives

Best times and quiz streaks are stored in the browser on your own device (localStorage). They are never uploaded, so clearing browser data resets them. Nothing personal is ever attached to them.

Content rules

  • News is written from real, linked sources — the source link stays on every article so grown-ups can check.
  • Frightening or graphic detail is rewritten out; scary topics are handled plainly, not avoided or sensationalised.
  • Every game has a suggested age band, listed on its page.

Screen-time friendly

Pages are light and load fast even on old computers and slow connections. Nothing autoplays, nothing pops up, nothing asks for "one more minute".

Questions or a content concern? Contact details live on the About page.

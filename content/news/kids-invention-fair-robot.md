At a school invention fair, a small wheeled robot quietly did its rounds, checking the soil of every plant in the classroom.

It was built by two students who got tired of plants wilting over the school holidays.

## How it works

A sensor in the robot's nose reads how moist the soil is. When the reading drops below a set level, the robot stops, lowers a tiny tube into the pot, and releases just enough water.

## What the judges said

The robot did not win first prize — that went to a door that opens by itself. But the plant robot won the award the students cared about most: the one for a project classmates actually asked to borrow.

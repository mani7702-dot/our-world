A library that looks like a tree has opened its doors, and children have been voting with their feet.

The building wraps around a living trunk, with reading nooks growing off it like branches. Some nooks are quiet, some have cushions, and one is reached by a small rope bridge.

## Books you borrow like fruit

Librarians say the busiest corner is the lowest branch, where picture books sit in baskets — easy to pick, easy to put back. Older readers climb one level higher, where the desks look out over the entrance hall.

## What the builders learned

The team says the trickiest part was not the tree at all — it was making the branches strong enough for children and quiet enough for reading. Both, it turns out, can be done with the same trick: thick, soft floorboards that muffle footsteps the way leaves do.

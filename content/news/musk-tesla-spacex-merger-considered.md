# What if Tesla cars and SpaceX rockets joined the same team?

## One person, two big companies

Imagine if the company that makes electric cars and the company that shoots rockets into space decided to become one. That is exactly what is being talked about.

Elon Musk — the entrepreneur who runs both **Tesla** (electric cars) and **SpaceX** (rockets and satellites) — said he thinks a merger could make sense. He made the comment during a call about SpaceX's latest results, and the news was reported by BBC News.

"Special committees" at both companies are studying the idea, but Musk was clear: nothing is decided yet. A merger would need **more than half of Tesla's shareholders** to vote yes, and that is a high bar.

## Why put them together?

The two companies are already friends. In 2026 they started **Terafab** — a joint project to build a huge factory that makes computer chips (the tiny brains inside phones, cars, and toys). Musk said the same teamwork could grow bigger.

Tesla makes electric cars and energy batteries. SpaceX launches rockets, runs the **Starlink** internet satellite network, and builds data centers for artificial intelligence. If they joined forces, one team could design a chip in the factory and test it in space the same week.

## How big would it be?

It would be one of the largest combinations in history. Think of it this way: if you added up what Tesla is worth to what SpaceX is worth, you get a number that is hard to even picture — trillions of dollars.

But Musk also joked that talking about a merger is like "tap dancing through a minefield" — it is complicated! Both companies have their own boards, their own shareholders, and their own goals.

## What happens next?

Nothing happens until the committees finish studying the idea and both sets of shareholders vote. Musk even said he will not sit on either committee, because he owns both companies — so he is officially a "conflicted party."

That means this story is still being written. Will rockets and cars share a garage? Maybe. But for now, it is just a big, exciting "what if."

## Fun Fact

SpaceX's Starlink satellites — thousands of them — already beam internet to remote places on Earth. The revenue from Starlink is now the biggest source of income for SpaceX, showing how space business can connect to everyday life.

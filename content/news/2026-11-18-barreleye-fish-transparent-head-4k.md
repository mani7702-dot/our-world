Imagine a fish with a head you can see straight through. Inside, two green tubes float where its eyes should be.

That is not a cartoon. It is a real deep-sea animal called the barreleye fish, and scientists have just filmed one in sharp 4K for the first time.
## A window in the top of its head

The barreleye fish lives far down in the ocean, in a dim layer called the twilight zone. Sunlight barely reaches it.

Its head has a clear, jelly-like dome on top. The dome is filled with fluid, so you can look right through it and see the eyes of the fish sitting inside.

Those eyes are not round. They are two long green tubes, and they point straight up.

## Eyes that look up, then swing forward

Pointing up is clever. Down in the dark, a small animal swimming overhead shows up as a dark shape against the faint glow from the surface. The barreleye watches for those shapes.

When it spots something worth eating, the eyes swivel forward inside the dome. Now the fish can keep its meal in view while it turns and swims after it.

The clear dome does a second job too. Some deep-sea animals, called siphonophores, dangle stinging tentacles. The barreleye likes to pick small crustaceans out of those tentacles, and the dome keeps its eyes safe while it does.

## Seen only eleven times in forty years

The team that filmed it works at the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute in California. They were exploring about 55 miles offshore when their robot submarine, called Doc Ricketts, found the fish nearly 2,000 feet down.

They stayed with it for about 20 minutes and recorded it in 4K.

That encounter was only the eleventh time the institute has seen a barreleye in almost 40 years and about 7,000 dives.

## Try this at home

Make your own twilight zone. In a dark room, shine a torch on a wall and hold a small toy in front of it so the toy is a dark shape against the light. That is what the barreleye sees overhead.

Then move the toy sideways and follow it with your eyes without turning your head. That is the swivel the fish does inside its dome.

## One more thing

The barreleye head is not a see-through skull. It is a clear bubble of tissue over the top of the head, like a little glass hat. The eyes underneath are the real thing.

Ninety baby turtles have made their first trip to the ocean, thanks to a team that spent months guarding their nests.

The team marked each nest, kept curious animals away, and watched for the tiny tracks that mean hatching has begun.

## The walk that matters most

When the babies emerge, everything depends on the short crawl from nest to surf. Lights near the beach can confuse them, so rescuers dim their torches and clear the path before the hatchlings set off.

## What happens next

Once in the water, the babies swim for open sea and are not seen again for years. Only a small number survive the journey — which is why guarded nests like these matter. When the females return as adults, they often choose the very same beach where they hatched.

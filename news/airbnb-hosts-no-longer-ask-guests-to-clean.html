Can Airbnb hosts still make guests strip beds and wash dishes?

By Our World Published 22 October 2026 at 17:39 IST
Reading options

Airbnb has updated its global house rules so hosts can no longer ask guests to do chores like stripping beds, washing dishes, or taking out trash — but they can still set seven standard rules plus reasonable requests.

Can Airbnb hosts still make guests strip beds and wash dishes?

Imagine arriving at a holiday rental after a long journey. You drop your bags, make yourself comfortable, and then — surprise — the host expects you to strip the beds and wash every dish from your stay. That was once common on Airbnb. But now, the rules have changed.

1. Seven standard rules, plus reasonable extras

Airbnb's new system lets every host choose seven standard house rules — things like no parties, no smoking, no loud music. On top of those, hosts can still add other "reasonable" requests. But the key word is reasonable.

The company said it heard from its community that the old rules were "confusing, onerous and hard to manage," so it redesigned them to be clearer from the start.

2. What guests no longer have to do

Under the updated policy, guests should not be asked to complete tasks that "require significant effort or time, or would typically be performed by a cleaner." That includes:

  • Stripping beds (removing used sheets)
  • Washing dishes and putting them away
  • Taking trash offsite
  • Deep-cleaning bathrooms or vacating furniture

If a host needs a property left in a certain condition, they can still ask — but the request must be reasonable, and guests should never be surprised by extra work after they've paid their cleaning fee.

3. When the changes start

The new house rules apply to all reservations booked on or after 20 November 2026. That gives hosts time to update their listings, and guests a clear picture of what to expect before they hit "reserve."

The goal is simple: a fairer stay for everyone. Guests pay a cleaning fee so they can relax — not scrub. Hosts get clearer expectations, and everyone knows the rules before the door is opened.

Try this at home

Next time you stay somewhere with a kitchen, try the "dish stack" trick: rinse your plate and bowl right after eating, then stack them neatly by the sink instead of leaving them scattered. It keeps things tidy with almost no effort — the spirit of Airbnb's new rule!

Fun Fact

Airbnb was founded in 2008 when two designers in San Francisco rent out their apartment couldn't afford rent, so they put up an airbed in their living room and made breakfast for guests. The company now has millions of hosts in almost every country on Earth — and now all of them have the same seven standard rules.

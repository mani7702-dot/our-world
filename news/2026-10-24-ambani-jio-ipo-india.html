"What is Jio Platforms' record-breaking IPO in India?"

By Our World Published 24 October 2026 at 07:24 IST
Reading options

"A huge Indian telecom company called Jio Platforms is going public with what could become India's biggest-ever stock market sale, aiming to raise billions to expand internet access and new technologies across the country."

1. What is happening with Jio Platforms?

A major Indian technology company called Jio Platforms is preparing to sell shares to the public for the first time. This is called an IPO — an "Initial Public Offering" — which means regular people can buy a small piece of the company and become part owners.

Jio Platforms hopes to raise up to 302 billion Indian rupees (that's about £2.4 billion). At its highest possible price, the company could be worth 10.3 trillion rupees — which would make it one of the most valuable companies in India!

2. Why is this important for India?

Jio Platforms makes it easier for millions of people in India to use the internet on their phones. When Jio first started in 2016, it gave people fast, affordable mobile data. Within just 170 days, 100 million people were using its services!

Before Jio, many families in India could not afford to get online. Jio changed that by starting a "price war" — meaning it made data so cheap that everyone could join in. Now, the company wants to use the money from its stock sale to build even better networks, including 5G and future 6G technology, and to explore new ideas like artificial intelligence and cloud computing.

3. Who is leading this big moment?

The company is part of Reliance Industries, one of India's biggest business groups. It was started by Mukesh Ambani, one of the world's wealthiest people. His son, Akash Ambani, now runs Jio Platforms and has been leading the company's growth.

Some of the world's most powerful investment companies — like BlackRock, Fidelity, and JPMorgan — have already promised to buy shares, showing that investors around the globe believe in Jio's future.

4. What does this mean for you?

When a company this big opens its doors to public investors, it shows how technology is growing every day. The money raised from the IPO will help bring faster internet and newer technology to more people — not just in India, but around the world, as digital tools and artificial intelligence become part of everyday life.

Try this at home

Want to understand how a company "goes public"? Grab a small treat (like a cookie) and break it into many tiny pieces. Each piece represents a "share." If you sold all the pieces to friends and family, you would be raising money — just like a company does in an IPO! The cookie company could then use that money to make even more cookies.

Fun Fact

Jio Platforms was only officially created as its own company in 2019, but it built on Jio's telecom business that started in 2016. In less than a decade, it grew from zero to being one of India's most valuable technology companies — faster than most companies grow in an entire century!

For grown-ups

"Jio Platforms is a subsidiary of Reliance Industries, the Indian multinational conglomerate founded by Mukesh Ambani. The IPO launched on October 22, 2026, with shares priced at 1,065-1,119 Indian rupees each, targeting a total valuation of up to 10.3 trillion rupees (£81 billion). The company aims to raise 302 billion rupees (£2.4 billion) by offering up to 270 million new shares. Jio launched its telecom services in September 2016 and gained 16 million subscribers in its first month, reaching 100 million within 170 days. The company plans to expand into artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and digital infrastructure. At the top of its price range, Jio would become India's fourth-largest company, behind Reliance Industries, Bharti Airtel, and HDFC Bank."

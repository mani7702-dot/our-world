Contact

Say Hello

We read everything. A correction, an idea, a question — all welcome.

Email us

Write to contact@ourworld.eu.org and we will answer in plain words. Anyone can write about anything at all — a young reader, a parent, a teacher or just a curious passer-by.

The message form

The message form on our About page is not switched on yet, so we have hidden it rather than let it fail silently. Until it is activated, please use email — it reaches us directly. When the form goes live, this page will say so.

Good things to write about

  • A correction — we got a fact wrong and you know the right one.
  • A topic you would like covered next.
  • A safety question, from a parent, teacher or a curious reader.
  • Just to say hello.

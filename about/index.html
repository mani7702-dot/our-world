About

About Our World

A small, calm corner of the internet for young readers — stories, games and news in one place.

Why it exists

Most kids' sites shout: pop-ups, ads, endless video autoplay, features designed to keep eyes glued. Our World does the opposite — it gives young readers good short content and then leaves them alone.

How it is built

The whole site is plain static files: no database, no accounts, nothing to hack. That is also why it loads fast on a school computer and keeps working when everything else is slow.

Contact

A contact email and a proper contact form arrive in the launch phase, together with the first live posts.
